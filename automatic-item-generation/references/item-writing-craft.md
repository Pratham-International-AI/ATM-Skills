# Item-Writing Craft — Universal Rules

These rules apply to every curriculum. A curriculum profile adds its own rules on top; where
a profile rule conflicts with a rule here, **the profile wins** (it encodes the curriculum's
actual conventions).

## Every item, every format

- **One concept per item** — never combine unrelated topics into a single question.
- **Difficulty matches marks** — a low-mark question must not require a high-mark depth of
  answer, and vice versa.
- **Unique intent within a batch** — no two items testing the same fact or concept.
- **Concise and direct** — no extra clauses, context, or hints that give away the answer.
- **Source-grounded** — every item and every value point traces back to the supplied source
  material; no outside facts, no invented events/characters/experiments.
- **Gradable** — every item carries value points (marking-scheme bullets) per the profile's
  marking rubric. No exceptions, even for "obvious" MCQ or fill-blank answers.

## MCQ craft

- **Option symmetry (the most-violated rule):** all options must be equal in length, style,
  and specificity. The correct answer must never stand out by being longer, more detailed,
  or more technical than the distractors.
- **Plausible distractors:** base wrong options on real student misconceptions or common
  errors (for numerical items: wrong formula, missed conversion, sign error) — never on
  nonsense or made-up-sounding content.
- Options must be **mutually exclusive**.
- No item's answer may reveal or hint at another item's answer in the same set.
- MCQs suit recall and comprehension; a question that asks the student to *explain* a
  mechanism or principle is usually better written as a constructed-response item.

## Fill-in-the-blank craft

- The sentence must have a **unique, unambiguous** answer.
- Blank a **keyword** (name, term, number, place) — never a trivial word or a long phrase.
- If the answer is a multi-word term, blank the **entire** term — never split it (blank
  "Tyndall effect", not "Tyndall ___ effect"-style fragments).
- **Parallel-structure rule:** if the sentence contains parallel items ("created or
  destroyed"), blank both or neither — blanking one of a parallel pair gives the answer away.
- Keep the sentence short; extra context tends to hint the answer.

## Constructed-response craft (short/long answer)

- The expected answer must fit the length the profile assigns to the mark value.
- Keep the question focused enough to be answerable within that length — not vast or
  open-ended.
- For multi-part questions, label sub-parts (a), (b), (c) and state marks per sub-part.
- Long-answer items must not overlap in topic with short-answer items generated in the
  same set.
